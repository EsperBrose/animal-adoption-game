# 🐾 Animal Adoption Game

A cozy **boxy 3D** browser game: turn into any animal you want (or a human!), make a family love you, and get adopted.

**Play it:** https://esperbrose.github.io/animal-adoption-game/

## What you can do
- **Become any animal** at the magic pond: 32 boxy animals, or type any animal you can think of. Pick a name, a color, and baby or adult. Animals are their real-life size.
- **Turn into a human** and back with one button (girl, boy or other, with your own skin, hair and clothes).
- **Get adopted**: do tricks, cuddles and play to fill a family's ❤️ hearts. When they're full, the family picks you up and carries you home.
- **Your home**: go inside, buy furniture and hats at the town store with coins you find and earn, decorate your room, and eat, sleep and play.
- **Explore**: the town, the forest, and a flight on the airplane to **Sunny Island**, with its palm trees and wild animals.
- Your game **saves automatically** in your browser.

## Controls
- Walk: arrow keys / WASD, or tap the ground
- Hop: Space
- Use doors, the store and the plane: E (or the button that appears)
- Tricks, cuddles, play with a family: 1, 2, 3
- Turn furniture while decorating: R

## Development
Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with [Three.js](https://threejs.org/) from a CDN, so there's nothing to install. GitHub Pages serves `main`.
See [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) for the phases and milestones. Opening the game with `?debug` adds test helpers.
